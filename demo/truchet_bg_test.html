Attributes:

  • data-mode: "light" or "dark"
  • data-brand: "m5t" (reserved for future use)
  • data-refresh: Animation refresh interval in milliseconds (default: 800)
  • data-cell-width: Width of each grid cell in pixels (default: 100)
